# Scene01 Hero Outfit Concepts (v2 — Scandi-studio, home/casual)

3 concepts, casual/home register to match the new environment. Reuses env
ramps (oak-light, sage-green, textile-grey, wall-warmwhite) where sensible;
each adds max 2 new accent colours not in the env palette.

## Concept 1 — "Loungewear"
Top: oversized textile-grey knit sweater (reuses env textile-grey ramp).
Bottom: warm-neutral oak-adjacent joggers (reuses oak-light ramp, darkest step).
Shoes: cream canvas slip-ons (new colour: soft cream #EDE6D6).
Accent: small sage-green hair tie / wrist accessory (reuses sage-green ramp).
New colours: 1 (cream shoes). Reads calm, blends with sofa/bedding — good
for idle/interact poses in the lounge zone.

## Concept 2 — "Kitchen casual"
Top: wall-warmwhite fitted tee (reuses env warm-white ramp).
Bottom: dusty terracotta apron-style skirt or rolled trousers (new colour:
terracotta #C97B55, a warm accent that pops against the oak/white kitchen).
Shoes: accent-black simple flats (reuses env accent-black ramp).
Accent: terracotta carries through as a thin belt/cuff for cohesion.
New colours: 1 (terracotta). Reads warm against kitchen run, distinct
silhouette from Concept 1 so it reads at a glance during kitchen interactions.

## Concept 3 — "Outdoor-ready" (entry/going out)
Top: sage-green utility jacket, open over a wall-warmwhite base layer
(reuses env sage-green + warm-white ramps).
Bottom: textile-grey straight trousers (reuses env textile-grey ramp).
Shoes: new colour mustard-yellow sneakers (#D9A441) — the one strong pop
colour, placed low on the sprite so it reads at 128px without overloading
the palette.
Accent: mustard carries to a small bag/strap detail for balance.
New colours: 1 (mustard). This is the "leaving via entry partition" outfit —
strongest silhouette contrast against the white/oak entry zone.

## Palette discipline
- All 3 concepts stay within the 2-new-colour cap.
- Total new hero-only colours across all 3: cream #EDE6D6, terracotta
  #C97B55, mustard #D9A441 — 3 distinct accents, each used in exactly one
  concept, so Krit can pick one without the other two polluting the final
  palette.
- No outfit reuses the old environment's wardrobe palette (prior scene is
  deprecated per brief).
